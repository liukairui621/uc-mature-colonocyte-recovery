# Manuscript style, citation and display-item audit

## Narrative

The manuscript now uses a direct IMRAD structure. The Background defines mucosal epithelial recovery, the existing evidence and the unresolved composition-versus-state question. Methods contains the study design, datasets, score construction, preprocessing and statistical procedures. Results reports observed sample sizes, estimates, intervals and P values. Discussion interprets the findings, compares them with prior epithelial and treatment-response studies, states the study's increment and explains its practical use.

The central narrative is: a six-gene mature absorptive colonocyte score rises with infliximab-associated endoscopic healing; longitudinal single-cell analysis localizes a substantial part of this signal to expression recovery within the released CT-colonocyte compartment; future remitters start from a lower score and converge toward similar follow-up levels; and the clinical-response validation distinguishes this mucosal tissue signal from a broader clinical endpoint.
